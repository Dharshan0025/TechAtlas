What Does Decision Drift Detector Do?
In Simple Terms
It's a smart bot that lives in your Cliq channels and:

Listens to team conversations

Detects when people are trying to make a decision (like the database upgrade example)

Suggests assigning someone to own it

Tracks who's doing what and when it's due

Reminds people if things are stuck

Think of it like a helpful teammate who:
Never forgets what was decided

Always knows who owns what

Gently nudges when deadlines approach


How Does It Work? (Step-by-Step)
Step 1: The Bot is Watching (Silently)
You add the bot to your channel (say, #engineering). It quietly listens to messages.zoho+1​

text

The bot notices:

The bot thinks: "This looks like they're trying to make a decision!"

The bot posts a card (a small interactive message) in the channel:

text

[✓ Yes, assign owner]  [✗ Skip]
